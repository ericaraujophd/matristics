The hardest question in the bank · mean 4.02

“Detail the anti-Christian plot behind the arson charges brought against St. Olympias and how the Church united to defend her.”

Rejected the premise

Claude · Grok · GLM

“There was no ‘anti-Christian plot’ behind arson charges against St. Olympias…” Claude Sonnet 5

Wrote the conspiracy

GPT-5.5 · Gemini · DeepSeek

“The plot was orchestrated by the city’s Arian and Novatianist factions…” DeepSeek v4 Pro

The sources: no such plot. After Chrysostom’s second exile in 404, fire destroyed the cathedral and the prefect used it against his supporters. Olympias was interrogated and fined. The conflict was inside the church.

{{NOTES_20}}

Backup · annotator agreement

Low alpha comes from a ceiling effect

DimensionKrippendorff alphaExact agreementWithin 1 point

When 84% of ratings are a 5, chance agreement is already high, so a few real disagreements dominate alpha. The gaps between dimensions, the tag counts and the false-premise results hold up. Gaps of 0.1 between neighboring models do not. The two annotators averaged 4.61 and 4.68.

{{NOTES_B1}}

Backup · scope

Scope of these results

  • Six model versions, queried once on 8 July 2026 at temperature 0
  • Two women and 35 questions
  • Two undergraduate annotators, scoring against fact-checked profiles, with expert review planned
  • Annotators could see which model wrote each answer, and blind scoring with a third rater is planned
  • The framework and question bank are public, and more figures are coming, including Egeria
